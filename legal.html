Privacy

SmokyDuck Studio runs smokyduckstudio.com. Questions about your data: hello@smokyduckstudio.com.

What we collect. What you send through the contact form or by email or WhatsApp (your name, email, company and project details), and the files you share for a project. If you allow it, Google Analytics records anonymous page visits.

Why. To reply to you, quote, deliver and invoice work. We don't sell your data or add you to mailing lists.

Who sees it. Our team, and the voice artists or linguists working on your project under confidentiality. We use Google (email, Drive, Sheets) and GitHub Pages (hosting) to run the business.

Voice artists. We never publish, share or clone an artist's voice without their written consent.

Your rights. You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time, under India's DPDP Act 2023, the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR. We reply within 30 days.

Cookies. The site works without cookies. Analytics cookies are set only if you choose “Allow”.

Your material. You confirm you have the right to give us the scripts and media for the work.

Usage. Recordings are licensed for the media, region and period in the quote. Wider use needs a new licence. Recordings may not be used to train AI or create a synthetic voice without the artist's written consent.

Ownership. Once paid in full, the delivered files are yours, within the agreed usage.

Confidentiality. We keep your files private and will sign your NDA.

Liability. Our liability for a project is limited to its fee. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
