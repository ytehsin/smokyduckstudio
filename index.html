( Who We Are )

SmokyDuck Studio is a Hindi dubbing studio and Indian language localisation company. Real voice artists and native linguists who make your content sound like it was made here — not translated.

( Human Studio )

Our Services

01

Voice-Over

Real human voice artists in every Indian language — for commercials, e-learning, explainers, documentaries, IVR and corporate films. Cast, directed and delivered as finished audio.

02

Dubbing

A Hindi dubbing studio that also dubs into every other Indian language. Human dubbing for films, series, animation and YouTube — performed by native voice actors, synced to picture and mixed for broadcast.

03

Translation

Professional translation and localisation between English and all Indian languages, by native linguists. Documents, websites, apps, scripts, courses and marketing — not just for voice-over.

04

Subtitling

Accurate, perfectly timed subtitles and clean transcripts in any Indian language, delivered with your project or on their own.
