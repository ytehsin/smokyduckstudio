( Services )

What we do.

Four services, one standard: it has to sound like it was made in Hindi.

01

Voice-Over

Natural Hindi, Urdu and English voices for commercials, e-learning, explainers, documentaries and corporate films. We cast, direct and deliver finished audio.

  • Commercials and brand films
  • E-learning and training
  • Explainers and corporate
  • Documentary narration
02

Dubbing

Hindi dubbing for animation, series, films and YouTube, including human finishing of AI dubs so they sound like they were made in Hindi.

  • Animation and character voices
  • Series, film and YouTube
  • AI-dub review and re-voice
  • Lip-sync where needed
03

Translation

Scripts adapted for the ear, not word-for-word. We translate for timing, tone and audience, whether Hindi or Hinglish.

  • English ⇄ Hindi scripts
  • Course and e-learning content
  • On-screen text
  • Glossaries for consistency
04

Subtitling

Accurate, perfectly timed subtitles and clean transcripts, delivered with your dub or on their own.

  • Hindi and English subtitles
  • SRT and VTT files
  • Transcription
  • Burned-in captions for social

( Questions )

How do you price?

Per finished minute for voice-over and dubbing, per word for translation. Send the script or a clip and we'll quote the same day where we can.

How fast can you deliver?

It depends on length and the number of voices. Short spots can turn around in a day or two. Your quote states the date.

Do you use AI?

Where it saves you time, yes. Every minute is still checked by a native Hindi speaker, and anything that doesn't sound right is re-recorded by a person.

Can we start with a short test?

Yes. A short sample is the best way to agree what good sounds like before a larger project.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before you share any files.

Who owns the recordings?

You do, once paid, for the usage agreed in the quote. We never clone or reuse a voice without the artist's written consent.
