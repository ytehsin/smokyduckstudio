( Services )

What we do.

Two studios. Our human studio — real voice artists and native linguists in every Indian language. And a separate AI studio for speed and scale.

( Human Studio )

Real people.

01

Voice-Over

Real human voice artists in every Indian language — for commercials, e-learning, explainers, documentaries, IVR and corporate films. Cast, directed and delivered as finished audio.

  • Commercials and brand films
  • E-learning and training
  • Documentary narration
  • IVR and announcements
02

Dubbing

A Hindi dubbing studio that also dubs into every other Indian language. Human dubbing for films, series, animation and YouTube — performed by native voice actors, synced to picture and mixed for broadcast.

  • Films, series and OTT
  • Animation and character voices
  • YouTube and digital content
  • Lip-sync and voice matching
03

Translation

Professional translation and localisation between English and all Indian languages, by native linguists. Documents, websites, apps, scripts, courses and marketing — not just for voice-over.

  • Indian language localisation
  • E-learning localisation
  • Websites, apps and documents
  • Marketing and transcreation
04

Subtitling

Accurate, perfectly timed subtitles and clean transcripts in any Indian language, delivered with your project or on their own.

  • Subtitles in all Indian languages
  • SRT and VTT files
  • Transcription
  • Burned-in captions for social

( Questions )

Which languages do you work in?

All Indian languages — including Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi and Assamese — plus English. Tell us the pair you need.

Are your voice-overs done by real people?

Yes. Our voice-over and dubbing services use real human voice artists only. AI voices are a separate service that you choose explicitly.

What's the difference between your human and AI services?

Human services are performed by voice artists and linguists. AI services are faster and cheaper, for projects where speed and volume matter more than performance. They are quoted separately, and we never mix them without telling you.

Do you localise e-learning courses?

Yes. E-learning localisation is a core service: translated course content, quizzes and on-screen text, plus human voice-over and subtitles, in any Indian language.

Do you translate without voice-over?

Yes. Translation is a service on its own — documents, websites, apps, courses and marketing in any Indian language.

How do you price?

Voice-over and dubbing per finished minute, translation per word. Send the script or file and we'll quote the same day where we can.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before you share any files.

Who owns the recordings?

You do, once paid, for the usage agreed in the quote. We never clone or reuse a voice without the artist's written consent.
