Who we are.

SmokyDuck Studio – Indic & Global Multimedia Localization.

( Our Story )

Bringing languages and voices closer to their audiences.

Founded in Agra, SmokyDuck Studio brings languages and voices closer to audiences across India and around the world. With recording studios in Agra, Delhi and Mumbai, we combine professional voice artists and native-language linguists to create content that sounds natural, connects culturally and speaks directly to its intended audience.

From translation and localisation to voice-over, dubbing and subtitling, we offer end-to-end language solutions for TV commercials, animation, e-learning, corporate films and digital content. Whether it's adapting a message into another language or bringing a character to life in a different voice, we make sure nothing gets lost in translation.

( What We Believe )

01

Human voices first

Our voice-over and dubbing are performed by real artists. A voice carries emotion, timing and culture that an audience feels.

02

Every Indian language

Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Assamese and more, plus English, each handled by native speakers.

03

Human and AI, kept apart

Our AI services are a separate offering for speed and scale. We never mix them into human work without telling you.

04

Artists come first

We never publish, share or clone an artist's voice without their written consent, and every recording is licensed for an agreed use.

( How We Work )

1. Brief

Send the script, video or file, the languages and your deadline.

2. Cast & plan

We match voices or linguists to your project and confirm price and timeline.

3. Record & translate

Directed recording sessions and native translation, synced to picture where needed.

4. Check & deliver

A second native speaker reviews everything before delivery in the formats you need.
